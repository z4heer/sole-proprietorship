## 1. Key ITR-3 & Trading Tax Changes Applicable for Filing (AY 2026–27)

1. **Revised Capital Gains Rates & Exemption Limits:**
* **STCG (u/s 111A):** Taxed at **20%** (increased from 15%).
* **LTCG (u/s 112A):** Taxed at **12.5%** (reduced from 10%), with the annual tax-exempt threshold raised to **₹1.25 Lakhs** (up from ₹1 Lakh).
* **STT Deduction Prohibition:** STT **cannot** be claimed as a transfer expense under Capital Gains (Schedule CG). However, non-STT costs (brokerage, exchange charges, stamp duty, GST) remain 100% deductible.


2. **Business Income (F&O & Intraday Equity):**
* Classified as **Business Income** (Non-Speculative for F&O, Speculative for Intraday). Taxed at your applicable slab rate.
* **Full Expense Deduction:** All direct costs (including STT/CTT) and indirect overheads (software, internet, laptop depreciation, research, audit fees) are **100% deductible** under Section 37(1) against F&O and Speculative business income.


3. **Turnover n Audit Rules (u/s 44AB):**
* **ICAI 8th Edition Turnover Method:** FnO turnover = $\sum \vert{}\text{P\nL}\vert{}$ (Absolute sum of profits and losses).
* **Tax Audit Threshold:** For 100% digital transactions (which applies to stockbroking), the audit limit is **₹10 Crores**. Since your aggregate trading turnover is **~₹10.02 Lakhs**, you are **exempt from a mandatory tax audit**.


4. **New Tax Regime Slabs (Default Regime):**
* Basic exemption limit is **₹4,00,000**.
* **Section 87A Rebate:** If total taxable income (excluding special rate gains) is up to **₹12,00,000**, your effective net tax liability under the normal slab becomes **Nil**.
