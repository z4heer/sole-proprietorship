Here is a comprehensive list and standard template format for calculating Turnover, P&L, and Expenses for filing **ITR-3** under Indian Income Tax provisions:

---

## 1. Summary Calculation Template

Use this primary schedule to summarize data across all four trading heads before filling out ITR-3 schedules (Schedule CG and Schedule BP):

| Trading Category | Income Tax Head | Turnover Definition / Formula | Gross P&L Formula | Allowable Deductions / Expenses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Long Term Trades (LTCG)** | Capital Gains (u/s 112A) | Total Sale Consideration (Gross Sales) | Gross Sale Price − Cost of Acquisition | Brokerage, Stamp Duty, Transfer Fee *(STT Not Allowed)* |
| **2. Short Term Trades (STCG)** | Capital Gains (u/s 111A) | Total Sale Consideration (Gross Sales) | Gross Sale Price − Cost of Acquisition | Brokerage, Stamp Duty, Transfer Fee *(STT Not Allowed)* |
| **3. Speculation (Intraday Equity)** | Business Income (Speculative u/s 43(5)) | Sum of Absolute values of Profits & Losses | Net Sum of Realized Profits & Losses | STT, Brokerage, Charges, Advisory, Software, Depreciation, Interest |
| **4. F&O (Futures & Options)** | Business Income (Non-Speculative) | Sum of Absolute values of Profits & Losses (ICAI 8th Ed.) | Net Sum of Realized Profits & Losses | STT, Brokerage, Charges, Advisory, Software, Office Rent, Interest |

## 2. Detailed Category Breakdown & Formulas

### Category A: Delivery Equity (Capital Gains)

> **Note:** Delivery trades are treated as Capital Gains unless you explicitly declare yourself as a full-time trader treating delivery equity as business stock-in-trade.

```text
[Long Term (LTCG) / Short Term (STCG)]

  Gross Sales Value (Total Sale Consideration)
- Cost of Acquisition (Purchase Value / Grandfathered Value u/s 112A)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
= Gross Capital Gain / Loss

- Transfer Expenses (Brokerage + Stamp Duty + Exchange Charges)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
= Net Capital Gain / Loss (Report in Schedule CG)

```

* **STT Exception:** Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is **not allowed** as a deduction under Section 48 for Capital Gains.

---

### Category B: Speculation Trades (Intraday Equity)

Intraday stock trades where delivery is not taken are classified as **Speculative Business Income**.

```text
Intraday Turnover Calculation:
Turnover = Σ | Profit or Loss per trade |
(Example: +₹5,000 profit on Trade A and -₹3,000 loss on Trade B = ₹8,000 Turnover)

Net Speculative P&L:
Net P&L = Total Realized Gains - Total Realized Losses

  Gross Realized P&L
- Direct Trading Expenses (STT, Brokerage, Exchange Charges, Stamp Duty, GST)
- Proportionate Overhead Expenses (Software, Internet, Advisory)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
= Net Speculative Business Income / Loss (Report in Schedule BP - Speculative)

```

---

### Category C: Futures & Options (F&O)

F&O trading is classified as **Non-Speculative Business Income** under Section 43(5).

#### Turnover Calculation Rules (As per ICAI 8th Edition Guidance Note):

* **Futures:** Absolute sum of all positive and negative differences ($\sum \vert{}\text{P\&L}\vert{}$).
* **Options:** Absolute sum of all positive and negative differences ($\sum \vert{}\text{P\&L}\vert{}$). *(Premium received on sale of options is only added if not already factored into the net P&L calculation by your broker)*.

```text
F&O P&L & Expense Calculation:

  Gross Realized P&L (Futures + Options)
- Direct Trading Charges (STT + Brokerage + Turnover Fees + Stamp Duty + SEBI Charges + GST)
- Indirect Business Expenses (Software, Data API, Internet, Advisory, Interest, Depreciation)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
= Net Non-Speculative Business Income / Loss (Report in Schedule BP)

```

## 3. Expense Eligibility Matrix for Business Incomes (Speculation & F&O)

Expenses can be deducted against **Speculative** and **F&O** income provided they are incurred wholly and exclusively for trading operations:

| Expense Head | Capital Gains (LTCG / STCG) | Speculation (Intraday) | F&O Trades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Securities Transaction Tax (STT)** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Brokerage & Exchange Turnover Fees** | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **GST, Stamp Duty, SEBI Charges** | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Trading Tools / Charting Software (e.g., TradingView)** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Internet & Mobile Bills (Proportionate)** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Advisory / Research / Course Fees** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Interest on Capital Borrowed for Trading** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |
| **Depreciation on Laptop / Computer (40%)** | ❌ Not Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed |

## Key Tax Compliance Checklist

1. **Tax Audit Thresholds (u/s 44AB):**
* Tax audit applies if total business turnover (F&O + Speculation) exceeds **₹10 Crores** (for 100% digital transactions).
* If turnover is below ₹2 Crores and you declare losses or profit less than 6% of turnover while total income exceeds the basic exemption limit, tax audit provisions may be triggered.


2. **Loss Carry-Forward (Schedule CFL):**
* **F&O Losses:** Can be set off against any non-salary income in the current year and carried forward for **8 years** to set off against future business profits.
* **Speculative Losses:** Can only be set off against speculative profits and carried forward for **4 years**.
* **Mandatory Requirement:** You must file your return on or before the due date (July 31 for non-audit cases) to carry forward losses.
