The **official changes for AY 2026-27 explicitly say that ITR-3 now has separate disclosures for:**

* Equity F&O
* Intraday
* **Commodity trading**
* Currency trading ([moneycontrol.com][1])

So you're right:

> **Commodity derivatives are not new. Millions of traders use MCX every year. The portal should absolutely support them.**

## My hypothesis

I now suspect the issue is **not with ITR-3**, but with the **online portal UI**.

Notice what you've observed:

* Trading Account shows **Intraday** and **F&O**.
* There is **no Commodity field**.
* BP derives figures only from those entries.
* Yet the official release notes say **commodity must be disclosed separately**. ([moneycontrol.com][1])

Those two facts don't fit together.

That usually means one of these:

1. **The online form has a UI defect or incomplete implementation.**
2. The commodity field is conditionally displayed only under certain business codes or other selections.
3. The **Excel/Common Offline Utility** exposes the field correctly while the online UI does not.

# I would do this instead of guessing

## Option 1 (my preferred recommendation)

Download the **Common Offline Utility (ITR-3)** from the Income Tax portal.

The official utility is available for AY 2026-27 and generates the same JSON that you upload to the portal. ([incometax.gov.in][2])

* It follows the official schema.
* It often exposes all fields defined by the schema.
* If the online UI has a defect, the offline utility may not.
