### **4. Key ITR-3 Filing Guidelines for Your Case**

1. **Set-off & Carry Forward of Losses (Schedule CFL):**
* **F&O Net Business Loss (₹773,163.43):** Can be set off against any income other than salary in the current year. Unadjusted losses can be **carried forward for 8 assessment years**.
* **Intraday Speculative Profit (+₹4,372.38):** Fully set off against F&O business losses or taxed as speculative profit.
* **LTCG Loss (-₹430,395.35):** Can only be set off against future LTCG (carried forward for 8 years).
* **STCG Loss (-₹17,297.50):** Can be set off against STCG/LTCG in future years (carried forward for 8 years).


2. **Tax Audit Requirement (u/s 44AB):**
* Total aggregated business turnover (F&O + Speculation) is **~₹10.02 Lakhs**, which is well below the statutory limit of **₹10 Crores** for digital transactions. A tax audit is not mandatory unless declared otherwise under specific provisions.
